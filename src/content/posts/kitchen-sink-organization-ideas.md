A kitchen sink sees more repeated use than most surfaces. An organized sink should make dishes easier to wash, tools easier to dry and the counter easier to wipe. The best layout is the one you can restore after dinner in a few minutes.

## 1. Separate wet work from dry storage

Clear a place beside the basin for washing tools and reserve another area for clean, dry items. Do not set freshly washed dishes next to a used sponge. Keep hand soap and dish soap accessible without lining the entire back edge of the sink with bottles.

## 2. Choose one caddy that drains

A [stainless steel OXO sink caddy](/shop/oxo-stainless-steel-sink-caddy/) is one possible way to group a sponge and brush. Measure the usable counter behind or beside the sink and check drainage and care instructions. Empty any collected water, clean the holder regularly and let tools dry between uses.

## 3. Keep the faucet area clear

Allow the faucet handle to move freely. Test whether you can wipe the surface with one pass of a cloth without shifting multiple containers. If the caddy blocks this motion, relocate it or choose a smaller holder.

## 4. Put refills somewhere else

Keep unopened dish soap and replacement sponges in a nearby cupboard rather than on the counter. Store cleaners in their original labeled containers and follow their instructions. Keep hazardous products away from children and pets; never mix cleaning products.

## 5. Reset after the final wash

Rinse loose food from the sink, clean the surface using an appropriate cleaner, empty the caddy’s drip area and hang damp cloths so they can dry. Replace worn cleaning tools as needed. A tidy sink area is a habit supported by storage, not a collection of matching accessories.

## A small-sink layout example

For a sink with almost no side ledge, keep a single washing tool and soap near the faucet and store extra cloths in a nearby dry drawer. If the caddy blocks the handle or interferes with wiping the backsplash, try the opposite side or a nearby tray. Choose a location where accumulated water can be emptied easily. Let cloths dry fully between uses, and keep separate tools for dishes and dirty surfaces. A clear basin and free faucet handle are better signs of a successful layout than matching bottles.
