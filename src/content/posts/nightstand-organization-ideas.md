The nightstand is the last surface you see at night and often the first you use in the morning. Instead of decorating it to match a photo, arrange it to support the things you do before sleep and when you wake.

## 1. Empty it and list your real essentials

Put everything into a temporary box. Return only the items you use in bed or immediately after waking. Glasses, a book and water may be enough. Put receipts, cosmetics and unopened mail where they belong.

## 2. Choose lighting you can reach

Position a lamp so you can switch it on without stretching or knocking objects. A [compact table lamp](/shop/yarra-decor-touch-control-lamp/) may work if the base fits and the cord reaches safely. Keep the lamp and charging equipment away from water and bedding.

## 3. Create a small catchall

A shallow dish or [wooden tray](/shop/hanobe-round-wood-tray/) can contain a watch or glasses. Choose the smallest one that holds those items and keep the rest of the nightstand open.

## 4. Use one drawer for quiet storage

If the nightstand has a drawer, separate things by use rather than by color. A small divider or box can keep spare accessories from sliding around. An [IRIS desktop drawer organizer](/shop/iris-usa-compact-desktop-organizer/) is a possible alternative on a stable nearby shelf if the nightstand has no drawers; check the variant dimensions.

## 5. Route the charging cable

Keep the cable where it is accessible but not looped across your walking path. Use the charger according to its instructions and avoid pinching cords under furniture or covering charging equipment with bedding.

## 6. Move backups away from the bed

Keep extra tissues, spare cables and unopened products in a closet or dresser. Do not store medications or small objects where children or pets can reach them. A bedside surface should not become overflow storage.

## 7. Close the day with a reset

Put the book back, collect the empty glass and return accessories to their tray. If an item repeatedly appears on the surface, decide whether it belongs in the nightly routine or needs a new home.

## A shared bedroom example

If two people use the same nightstand, agree on one spot for shared objects and a small personal container for each person rather than letting belongings mingle. A shallow tray can hold glasses or a watch; a deeper drawer is better for spare cables. Leave enough clear room to reach a glass of water without brushing against the lamp. Keep charging devices on a firm ventilated surface, away from bedding. If your table is already too crowded, moving backups elsewhere may be more useful than adding a new organizer.
