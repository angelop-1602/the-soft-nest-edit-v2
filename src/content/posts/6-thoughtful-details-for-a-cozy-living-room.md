## How to bring the six details together

The trick is to **repeat materials and colors without repeating every object**. Begin with the rug and seating arrangement, then layer in textiles, light and one or two accents. The wooden tray can hold a small decorative grouping; the vases can work just as well on a separate shelf; and the olive tree adds height away from the main seating area.

Here's a simple order to try before buying anything new:

1. **Edit the room first.** Put away accessories that make your surfaces feel crowded.
2. **Look at the light.** Reposition an existing lamp before shopping for a new one.
3. **Layer what you already have.** Try a different cushion arrangement and fold a throw over the sofa arm.
4. **Test the scale.** Use painter's tape for a potential rug and measure the empty corner for a plant.
5. **Finish with a focal point.** Choose a tray *or* sculptural vase display, not necessarily both on the same surface.

A cozy room should still work for real life. Keep the coffee table usable, preserve walking space and choose textiles you can care for easily. Small changes usually feel more satisfying when they make the room more comfortable—not just more photogenic.
