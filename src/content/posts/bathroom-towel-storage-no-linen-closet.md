Without a linen closet, towels need two different homes: a dry, clean place for folded reserves and a ventilated place for towels currently in use. Trying to solve both with a single basket often makes the bathroom feel crowded.

## 1. Count the towels you actually need

Start with the household’s normal laundry routine. Keep enough clean towels to cover the next wash, then relocate rarely used extras. Do not add another storage unit before checking how much fabric actually needs a home.

## 2. Use a dry reserve zone

A shelf in a bedroom cupboard or a clean, dry cabinet can hold folded towels outside the bathroom. Keep them away from leaks, damp toiletries and cleaning products. Stack them at a height you can reach without pulling a whole pile down.

## 3. Give damp towels a drying location

A towel bar or compatible [over-the-door hooks](/shop/optish-over-the-door-hooks/) can work if the towels can spread out and the door closes freely. Follow mounting and load instructions. Do not block a door or pile wet towels in a closed container.

## 4. Use baskets for dry textiles only

A [woven storage basket](/shop/oiahomy-woven-blanket-basket/) is a potential place for clean rolled towels if the selected basket is appropriate for the room and stays dry. Check dimensions and material. A basket you already own may work just as well; avoid storing damp towels inside it.

## 5. Create a laundry-day routine

When a clean towel comes out, take the used one to laundry or hang it to dry as appropriate. Check for dampness before folding. Keeping the reserve and active towels separate makes a small bathroom easier to maintain than trying to squeeze every towel onto the same shelf.

## Plan for drying as well as storing

Divide towels into those in use, those waiting for laundry and fully dry clean reserves. A basket is useful for clean reserves in a dry room, but it should not become a place for damp towels. In a shared bathroom, give each person a separate hook or drying bar and check whether towels can hang without overlapping too much. If the bathroom stays humid, use a dry shelf outside the room for backups. Rotate the spare towels on laundry day so the same stack does not sit untouched.
