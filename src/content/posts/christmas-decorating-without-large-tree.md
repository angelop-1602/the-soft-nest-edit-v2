A large Christmas tree is not the only way to make a small home feel festive. Choose two or three places where seasonal greenery, a warm light and a quiet color palette can create atmosphere while leaving the room easy to live in.

## 1. Pick a small focal point

Use a corner of a sideboard or an existing table rather than giving up an entire walking path. A compact decorative branch arrangement can draw the eye. Keep it stable and away from places where people reach for daily objects.

## 2. Work with the greenery you own

An existing [artificial olive tree](/shop/warmplants-artificial-olive-tree-4ft/) can remain part of the room’s year-round palette. Instead of attaching heavy ornaments, place a seasonal accent nearby. Check whether any lighting or decoration is suitable for the plant and keep it away from heat sources.

## 3. Make a tabletop arrangement

A [sculptural vase](/shop/lvases-snuggle-ceramic-vase-set/) can hold appropriate dry stems or simply stand alone. Check whether the vase is designed to hold water before using fresh greenery. Use a small tray to group objects only if it does not take over the functional table space.

## 4. Repeat warm colors through textiles

A cream [textured cushion](/shop/miulee-cream-slubby-pillow-covers/) paired with an olive or muted burgundy textile can suggest the season without requiring new themed cushions. Choose covers and throws that you can use after the holidays.

## 5. Keep lighting safe and easy to remove

Use suitable battery-operated candles or approved decorative lights, following their instructions. Avoid open flames near greenery, fabric and pets. Keep exits and cords clear. When the holiday ends, remove the small accents and return to the everyday room without having to store a large tree.

## A small-space decorating example

Use a vase of seasonal branches on a shelf, a small arrangement on the dining table or a wreath on a wall as the single focal point. Repeat a modest color accent on a cushion or ribbon instead of creating several competing displays. Keep enough space to eat, work and open cabinets. If the arrangement includes battery lights, check the battery compartment and follow the instructions. Keep loose greenery and textiles away from heat sources, and choose flameless candles where possible.
