A welcoming guest bathroom does not need a hotel-style display. Visitors should be able to find hand soap, a clean towel and toilet paper without opening unfamiliar cabinets. A simple arrangement is especially valuable when the room is small.

## 1. Clean first and leave a landing space

Clear the vanity, clean the surfaces and put your personal toiletries away. Leave a dry area where a guest can place a small toiletry bag. Check that the toilet, faucet and door work normally and that a wastebasket is easy to find.

## 2. Make handwashing obvious

Put soap beside the sink and a fresh hand towel within reach. A small [wooden vanity tray](/shop/zoocatia-wooden-vanity-tray/) can group soap and a washable dish if there is enough room; leave space for wiping around them. Avoid placing absorbent wood in standing water.

## 3. Offer only useful extras

A closed [bamboo-lid jar](/shop/vitever-bamboo-lid-bathroom-jars/) can hold clean cotton products in a dry area. Spare toilet paper should be easy to see, not hidden behind personal belongings. Keep cleaning products and medications out of guests’ and children’s reach.

## 4. Plan for towels and the door

Leave a place where a damp towel can dry. [Over-door hooks](/shop/optish-over-the-door-hooks/) may work when the door closes freely and the rack is rated for the load. Spread towels rather than stacking them on one hook. If the door is unsuitable, use an existing towel bar.

## 5. Finish with a simple visitor check

Stand at the entrance and imagine using the room for the first time. Can you see the soap, towel and spare paper? Is there a clear spot for your things? Remove heavy fragrance and unnecessary decor; a freshly cleaned, functional room is more useful than a crowded display.

## Do a visitor walk-through

Walk into the room as if it were unfamiliar: can you find soap, a dry hand towel, toilet tissue and a waste bin without opening several cabinets? Keep personal items and medications out of view and stored safely. Place spare tissue where it is easy to spot, rather than filling the counter with decorative bottles. If a guest is staying overnight, provide a separate dry landing space for their own toiletries. A clean surface and useful essentials usually matter more than an elaborate arrangement.
